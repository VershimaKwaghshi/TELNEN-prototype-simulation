Restitution and immediate Social Bond

When a trader reaches the 50% hard stop, the remaining 50% equity is not treated as a reason to wait. In this scenario, 90% of affected traders immediately use that remaining equity as collateral for a Social Bond. The restitution claim remains active while recovery from the responsible manager continues.

Capital Building

Example target: $5,000
Daily scheduled payment = $5,000 × 1.1% = $55
1% repayment component = $50
0.1% TE service fee component = $5
100 day scheduled cash flow = $5,500
100 day repayment component = $5,000
100 day TE service fee = $500
The simulator therefore shows the full 1.1% payment volume while keeping the 0.1% service fee identifiable as TE revenue.

Social Bond

Example Social Bond = $2,500
Activation fee = $2,500 × 20% = $500
Bonders = 40% of fee = $200
Facilitator = 40% of fee = $200
TE = 20% of fee = $100
The 4% figure is not presented as the Social Bond fee. It is only the effective TE share when the 20% activation fee and TE's 20% allocation are multiplied.

Withdrawal distribution example

The simulator treats TE's withdrawal revenue as 15% of the estimated total withdrawal volume. The withdrawal example also displays the current 35% owner, 35% manager and separate 15% referrer allocation. The remaining 15% is TE.
