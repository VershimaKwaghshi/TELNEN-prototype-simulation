Scale formula notes

The scale table treats the displayed assumptions as scenario inputs. Capital Building uses the full 1.1% scheduled payment for cash flow and separately identifies the 1% repayment and 0.1% TELNEN service fee. Broker rebate is modeled from daily lots multiplied by the selected number of trading days. Withdrawal volume is modeled from participating users and the selected percentage of average account size. Restitution stress is a scenario, not a forecast.
